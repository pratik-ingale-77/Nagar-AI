Meet
Nagar-AI.

Nagar-AI is an AI-powered civic complaint platform designed to make reporting everyday city problems easier, faster and smarter. Citizens can upload a photo of a civic issue, use AI to identify the problem, detect its location, generate a structured complaint and track it.

From potholes and garbage to broken streetlights, water leakage and damaged roads — Nagar-AI connects citizens with smarter civic reporting.

🇮🇳 Technology for a Better India
WHAT IS NAGAR-AI?

Making Civic Reporting Smarter

Nagar-AI simplifies the process of reporting civic problems by combining artificial intelligence, location services and structured complaint generation.

🤖

AI-Powered Detection

Upload a photo of a civic problem and Nagar-AI can analyze the image and identify the type of issue.

📍

Location Intelligence

Location services help identify where the civic issue was reported so that the complaint contains useful location information.

📝

Smart Complaints

Instead of writing everything manually, citizens can generate a structured complaint using the information collected by the platform.

🏛️

Department Routing

The identified category can help determine which civic department should handle the reported problem.

🎫

Complaint Tracking

Citizens can use a complaint ID to check the progress of a submitted civic complaint.

🇮🇳

Built for Indian Cities

Nagar-AI is designed around everyday civic challenges faced by citizens in Indian cities and communities.
