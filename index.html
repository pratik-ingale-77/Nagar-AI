What is Nagar-AI?

Nagar-AI connects citizens with smarter civic reporting. Instead of trying to figure out which department should receive a complaint, citizens can simply upload a photograph of the problem. Our AI analyzes the image and helps identify the civic issue, its priority and the appropriate department. The platform can then use the detected information to create a structured complaint.

🤖 AI Detection Understands what is actually visible in an uploaded image.
📍 Location Helps capture the location of the reported issue.
📝 Smart Reporting Converts information into a structured complaint.
🎫 Tracking Gives citizens a complaint ID for future tracking.
