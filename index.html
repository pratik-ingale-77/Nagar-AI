Nagar-AI is an AI-powered civic complaint platform designed to make reporting public problems faster, smarter and easier. Citizens can upload a photo of a civic issue, let AI identify the problem, detect the location and generate a structured complaint. From potholes and garbage to broken streetlights, damaged roads, drainage and water leakage — Nagar-AI helps connect citizens with civic authorities.
