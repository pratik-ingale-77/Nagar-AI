ABOUT NAGAR-AI

Technology for Better Civic Reporting

Nagar-AI is designed to make reporting everyday civic problems easier for citizens. Instead of figuring out which department to contact or writing a complicated complaint manually, a citizen can upload a photograph of the problem and let AI help analyze it.

The platform can identify the type of civic issue, estimate its priority, suggest the responsible department, capture the location and help generate a structured complaint. The goal is simple: make civic reporting faster, clearer and easier to track.

OUR MISSION

Making Civic Reporting Simpler for Everyone

Nagar-AI aims to bridge the gap between citizens and civic authorities by turning real-world problems into structured, understandable reports. By combining image analysis, location information and smart complaint generation, the platform can help citizens communicate civic issues more effectively.

From a pothole on a road to overflowing garbage or a broken streetlight, Nagar-AI is designed around one simple idea: when reporting a problem becomes easier, taking action can become easier too.
